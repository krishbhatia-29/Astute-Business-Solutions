WHO WE ARE

Structured recovery. Measurable portfolio outcomes.

Astute Business Solutions is a specialised debt recovery, collections, and verification services organisation in the financial services domain. The company supports banks, NBFCs, fintech companies, and financial institutions through compliant, process-driven recovery strategies designed to deliver consistent and measurable portfolio outcomes.

Powered by structured processes and technology-enabled operations, Astute Business Solutions delivers transparent reporting and consistently strong recovery performance across client portfolios.

01

Vision

To be a trusted and leading organization in recovery and verification services, defined by integrity, operational strength and consistent delivery of high quality financial outcomes.

02

Mission

To deliver structured, ethical, and intelligence-driven recovery solutions through disciplined processes and skilled professionals creating measurable performance standards.

SERVICES OFFERED

Focused capabilities across the recovery lifecycle.

01

Debt Recovery Services

Comprehensive recovery solutions across early-stage, mid-bucket, late-stage, and write-off portfolios, covering both secured and unsecured assets through controlled engagement practices.

02

Verification & Investigation

Credit profile verification, field investigations, and supporting checks conducted to validate borrower information and assist in informed credit and risk decisions.

03

Portfolio Management Support

Regular structured follow-ups, performance tracking, and client-aligned reporting ensure transparency, control and consistent outcomes across assigned portfolios.

OPERATIONAL PROCESS

A controlled six-stage operating model.

01

Portfolio Assessment & Segmentation

Accounts are categorized based on overdue duration, repayment history, and risk indicators to enable focused recovery planning.

02

Debtor Verification & Prioritization

Borrower information is verified, and high priority cases are identified for timely intervention.

03

Technology-Enabled Contact

Dialer-supported calling systems facilitate structured communication, real-time call tracking, and scheduled follow-ups.

04

Negotiation & Settlement Management

Ethical and customized repayment solutions are discussed, with structured settlements aligned to RBI and client guidelines.

05

Legal & Repossession Support

Statutory compliant legal recovery processes and repossession of secured assets are initiated where authorized.

06

Monitoring, Reporting & Client Review

Continuous performance tracking with regular reporting and review updates to ensure transparency and improvement.

COMPLIANCE & GOVERNANCE

Responsible execution is part of the recovery process.

We work within a clear compliance framework that follows relevant regulations and the policies set by our clients. All recovery and verification activities are carried out with strict compliance to legal, ethical, and procedural standards.

Our governance model emphasizes controlled communication, data privacy, and set processes. We regularly monitor these practices to ensure consistency and accountability in all our work.

OUR REACH

Localized field strength with nationwide communication reach.

Our operational model is designed to combine localized field strength with nationwide communication reach. Dedicated field teams operate across Delhi NCR, with extended on-ground coverage in select locations of Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand, ensuring faster physical verification, borrower visits, and resolution support wherever required.

Complementing field execution, our centralized tele-calling center in New Delhi enables pan-India outreach, allowing continuous borrower engagement, structured follow-ups, and coordinated recovery actions across multiple portfolios simultaneously.
